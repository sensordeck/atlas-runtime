## 4. Why Atlas?

Atlas provides:

- replay-backed runtime governance visibility
- deterministic replay admissibility
- limitation-preserved runtime interpretation
- cross-runtime governance comparison
- organizational timing trust visibility

Traditional runtime logs are often:

- fragmented
- non-replayable
- platform-specific
- inconsistent across teams
- difficult to compare across OEM/supplier boundaries

Atlas introduces:

- replay lineage
- governance windows
- admissibility boundaries
- limitation-preserved evidence interpretation
- organizational trust surfaces

Atlas augments existing runtime systems.

Atlas does NOT replace internal logging infrastructure.

---

## 5. Why Now?

Modern robotics systems increasingly depend on:

- multi-sensor synchronization
- distributed runtime timing behavior
- replay integrity
- cross-platform deployment consistency
- supplier integration trust

Timing governance problems become significantly harder after:

- fleet deployment
- platform divergence
- supplier escalation
- production rollout

Atlas exists to expose replay-visible governance deviations earlier.

---

## 6. Why Not Just Internal Logs?

Internal logs typically lack:

- deterministic replay lineage
- governance normalization
- cross-domain comparison
- admissibility preservation
- limitation-aware interpretation

Atlas provides:

- replay-backed governance visibility
- deterministic regeneration references
- organizational comparison surfaces
- replay admissibility boundaries

Replay admissibility
≠
causal proof.

中文说明：

Replay 证明的是“可重复重放一致性”，

## 7. Why Not Self-Build?

Equivalent governance infrastructure requires:

- replay normalization
- deterministic regeneration
- evidence lineage systems
- governance semantics
- comparison infrastructure
- limitation preservation
- admissibility governance
- organizational rendering surfaces

For most OEM organizations:

timing governance infrastructure is not the strategic product.

The strategic product is usually:

- perception
- autonomy
- robotics behavior
- AI systems
- deployment operations

Atlas externalizes governance plumbing complexity.

中文说明：

Atlas 的价值之一，
是将“非战略性的时序治理基础设施”
从 OEM 核心产品研发中解耦。
